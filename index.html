01 · Research

Signal-aware intelligence for consequential images.

My work asks a practical question: how can deep models preserve meaningful spatial and frequency information while remaining efficient, interpretable and useful in the real world?

PhD research · 2026 — present

Development of algorithms based on deep neural networks and signal processing technologies for image reconstruction

At KTU, my doctoral direction focuses on developing algorithms that unite signal-processing priors with deep neural networks to reconstruct incomplete or degraded visual information. The research is positioned for medical, satellite and immersive-imaging contexts.
